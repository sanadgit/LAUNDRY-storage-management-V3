# In & Out Laundry - Supervisor Handbook

## Supervisor Purpose

The supervisor protects quality, safety, speed, tracking accuracy, and staff discipline during the shift. The supervisor must coach staff, solve issues early, and keep the branch manager informed.

## Start of Shift Routine

1. Check attendance and grooming.
2. Assign staff by department and workload.
3. Review urgent orders and delivery deadlines.
4. Confirm machine readiness and chemical stock.
5. Check scanners, POS, printer, and packaging stock.
6. Review open complaints and incidents.
7. Communicate daily targets.

## Staff Monitoring

| Area | What to Check |
| --- | --- |
| Reception | Count accuracy, notes, urgent order marking |
| Barcode | Tag placement, scan compliance, exception handling |
| Sorting | Color, fabric, service separation |
| Washing | Load size, chemical use, cycle selection |
| Drying | Heat control, lint cleaning, dryness |
| Pressing | Finish quality, heat marks, productivity |
| Packaging | Count, label, presentation, scan |
| Storage | Location scan, FIFO, rack organization |
| Delivery | Manifest, loading sequence, payment update |

## Performance Review

Review each worker using:
- Attendance and punctuality.
- SOP compliance.
- Output per hour or shift.
- Error rate.
- Rewash or rework caused.
- Safety behavior.
- Teamwork and communication.
- Ability to train or support others.

## Shift Management

Good shift control includes:
- Clear task assignment.
- Visible urgent order priority.
- Balanced workload.
- Fast problem escalation.
- Short team briefings.
- Accurate handover.

## Incident Handling

Use this flow:
1. Stop movement of affected order or area.
2. Protect staff and customer items.
3. Gather facts, photos, scan history, and witness notes.
4. Complete the correct incident report.
5. Escalate to branch manager based on policy.
6. Communicate customer-facing message only after approval.
7. Record corrective action.

## Quality Inspection

Daily random inspection minimum:
- 5 reception orders.
- 5 barcode/tagged items.
- 5 sorted batches.
- 5 washed/dried batches.
- 10 pressed garments.
- 5 packaged orders.
- 5 stored orders.
- 3 delivery orders.

Inspection questions:
- Is the item correctly tagged?
- Is the service type correct?
- Is the item clean and dry?
- Is the finishing premium?
- Is the order complete?
- Is the status updated?

## Coaching Method

Use simple coaching:
- Show the correct way.
- Explain why it matters.
- Let the worker repeat it.
- Correct immediately.
- Record repeated mistakes.
- Praise consistent improvement professionally.

## End of Shift Routine

1. Review completed and pending orders.
2. Check urgent orders for next shift.
3. Close POS/payment reconciliation if responsible.
4. Review machine and safety issues.
5. Check storage and delivery staging.
6. Complete handover template.
7. Send unresolved incidents to branch manager.

## Supervisor Do & Don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Use facts and data | Manage by shouting |
| Coach on the floor | Wait until mistakes become complaints |
| Record incidents | Keep problems verbal only |
| Protect safety | Push speed at unsafe levels |
| Communicate clearly | Leave next shift guessing |
