# In & Out Laundry - Course Outline

## Course Purpose

This course trains employees, supervisors, and branch managers to run a clean, safe, fast, and traceable laundry operation across In & Out Laundry branches in the UAE.

## Target Learners

| Group | Main Focus |
| --- | --- |
| New employees | Basic workflow, safety, customer item handling |
| Production workers | Sorting, washing, drying, pressing, packaging |
| Reception staff | Customer handling, POS, tagging, complaint basics |
| Drivers | Pickup, delivery, mobile POS, handover |
| Supervisors | Monitoring, KPI, incident handling, quality checks |
| Branch managers | Full control, audits, service recovery, reporting |

## Recommended Course Duration

| Training Block | Duration |
| --- | --- |
| Company induction | 2 hours |
| Customer reception and POS | 4 hours |
| Tagging, sorting, washing, drying | 8 hours |
| Pressing, packaging, storage | 6 hours |
| Delivery and pickup | 3 hours |
| Safety, incidents, complaints | 5 hours |
| KPI and automation | 3 hours |
| Practical floor assessment | 1 to 3 shifts |

## Module Sequence

| Module | Title | Audience | Delivery Method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Company Introduction | All staff | Classroom and branch walk |
| 2 | Customer Reception Process | Counter, supervisor | Role-play and POS demo |
| 3 | Tagging & Barcode Workflow | All operations | Scanner demo and practice |
| 4 | Clothes Sorting Operations | Production | Floor demonstration |
| 5 | Washing Procedures | Wash team | Machine training |
| 6 | Blanket Processing System | Blanket team, storage | Large-item practical |
| 7 | Drying Procedures | Wash/dry team | Machine training |
| 8 | Ironing & Pressing | Pressing team | Live finishing demo |
| 9 | Packaging Standards | QC, packaging | Quality table practice |
| 10 | Storage & Warehouse Handling | Storage, dispatch | Rack and scan practice |
| 11 | Delivery & Pickup Workflow | Drivers, dispatch | Route and handover practice |
| 12 | POS System Usage | Reception, supervisors | System simulation |
| 13 | Lost or Damaged Items | Supervisors, counter | Case study |
| 14 | Complaint Handling | Counter, supervisors | Role-play |
| 15 | Hygiene & Safety | All staff | Safety walk and drill |
| 16 | Staff Professionalism | All staff | Briefing and policy review |
| 17 | KPI & Productivity | Supervisors, managers | Dashboard workshop |
| 18 | AI & Automation | All staff | System workflow demo |
| 19 | Emergency Procedures | All staff | Drill and scenario practice |

## Learning Methods

- Short classroom explanation.
- Real branch demonstration.
- Staff role-play.
- Before and after garment examples.
- Scanner and POS practice.
- Supervisor observation.
- Daily quiz questions.
- Practical sign-off.

## Assessment Plan

| Assessment | Passing Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Knowledge quiz | 80 percent |
| Practical workflow observation | No critical safety or tracking error |
| POS or scanner test | Correct process without supervisor correction |
| Quality sample check | Meets branch presentation standard |
| Safety check | PPE and emergency steps understood |

## Certification Levels

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Level 1 - Trainee | Can work only under supervision |
| Level 2 - Operator | Can complete assigned task independently |
| Level 3 - Senior Operator | Can train others on task |
| Level 4 - Supervisor Ready | Can monitor quality, safety, KPI, and incidents |

## Training Records

Record for each learner:
- Name and branch.
- Job role.
- Modules completed.
- Quiz score.
- Practical assessment result.
- Supervisor comments.
- Next training required.

## Visual Course Design

Recommended style:
- White and deep navy base.
- Teal or emerald accents for cleanliness and trust.
- Gold accent for premium service.
- Red only for safety warnings.
- Use real branch photos wherever possible.
- Use simple icons for each module.

Dashboard idea:
- Training completion by branch.
- Certified staff by department.
- Safety training expiry.
- Average quiz score.
- Practical assessment pass rate.
