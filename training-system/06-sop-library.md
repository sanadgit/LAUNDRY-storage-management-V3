# In & Out Laundry - SOP Library

## SOP 01 - Customer Reception

Purpose: Receive customer items accurately.

Steps:
1. Greet customer.
2. Confirm customer details.
3. Count all items.
4. Inspect stains, damage, buttons, pockets, and fabric risk.
5. Select service and delivery time.
6. Enter notes in POS.
7. Print receipt and labels.
8. Send to tagging/sorting.

Control points:
- Item count must match receipt.
- Special instructions must be written.
- Urgent order must be marked.

## SOP 02 - Barcode Tagging

Purpose: Give every item traceable identity.

Steps:
1. Match item with POS order.
2. Print barcode or QR label.
3. Attach in approved location.
4. Scan to confirm.
5. Move to next stage only after scan.

Control points:
- No untagged item.
- No unreadable label.
- Supervisor handles missing tag.

## SOP 03 - Sorting

Purpose: Protect fabrics and service accuracy.

Steps:
1. Scan into sorting.
2. Sort by service type.
3. Sort by color.
4. Sort by fabric and temperature.
5. Identify stains.
6. Separate delicate and risky items.
7. Route to correct processing area.

Control points:
- Whites separate from colors.
- Delicates protected.
- Stains marked before washing.

## SOP 04 - Washing

Purpose: Clean items safely and consistently.

Steps:
1. Check machine and batch.
2. Confirm fabric and soil level.
3. Load within machine weight limit.
4. Select correct cycle and temperature.
5. Use approved detergent/dosing.
6. Monitor cycle.
7. Remove and scan to next stage.

Control points:
- No overload.
- No chemical guessing.
- No wrong temperature.

## SOP 05 - Blanket Processing

Purpose: Process large items safely with full tracking.

Steps:
1. Scan blanket.
2. Inspect condition.
3. Weigh blanket.
4. Select correct machine.
5. Wash and dry completely.
6. Fold to standard.
7. Package and label.
8. Scan storage location.

Control points:
- Use trolley for heavy items.
- Do not pack damp blankets.
- Label must be visible.

## SOP 06 - Drying

Purpose: Dry items without damage.

Steps:
1. Clean lint filter.
2. Check fabric and load.
3. Select heat and time.
4. Monitor cycle.
5. Remove promptly.
6. Check dryness.
7. Send to pressing, folding, or packaging.

Control points:
- Low heat for delicate items.
- No damp packaging.
- Burning smell means stop and report.

## SOP 07 - Ironing and Pressing

Purpose: Finish garments to premium standard.

Steps:
1. Scan garment.
2. Check care label and stains.
3. Set heat and steam.
4. Press in correct sequence.
5. Hang or fold correctly.
6. Send to QC.

Control points:
- Do not press stains.
- Avoid shine marks.
- No burns or wrinkles.

## SOP 08 - Packaging

Purpose: Protect and present completed orders.

Steps:
1. Scan order.
2. Count items.
3. Final quality check.
4. Package by type.
5. Attach label.
6. Scan to storage or delivery.

Control points:
- Complete order only.
- Correct label only.
- Clean packaging only.

## SOP 09 - Storage

Purpose: Store completed orders for fast retrieval.

Steps:
1. Scan completed order.
2. Select rack/shelf.
3. Place item carefully.
4. Scan location.
5. Use FIFO.
6. Retrieve by customer/order verification.

Control points:
- No floor storage.
- No unscanned location.
- Heavy items on lower shelves.

## SOP 10 - Pickup and Delivery

Purpose: Complete customer handover accurately.

Steps:
1. Prepare manifest.
2. Scan orders before loading.
3. Load by route sequence.
4. Keep clean and dirty items separate.
5. Verify customer at handover.
6. Collect or confirm payment.
7. Update delivery status.

Control points:
- No order leaves without scan.
- Wrong address risk must be checked.
- Failed delivery reason recorded.

## SOP 11 - POS Order Creation

Purpose: Maintain accurate system record.

Steps:
1. Login with personal user.
2. Select customer.
3. Enter item, service, price, notes, and delivery time.
4. Apply approved discount if any.
5. Confirm with customer.
6. Print receipt and labels.

Control points:
- No shared passwords.
- No unauthorized discounts.
- Notes must be clear.

## SOP 12 - Lost or Damaged Item

Purpose: Handle incidents professionally.

Steps:
1. Stop order movement.
2. Inform supervisor.
3. Check scan history.
4. Photograph damage if any.
5. Complete incident report.
6. Escalate and communicate by policy.
7. Close with corrective action.

Control points:
- Do not hide incidents.
- Do not promise compensation without approval.
- Keep evidence.

## SOP 13 - Complaint Handling

Purpose: Resolve customer issues professionally.

Steps:
1. Listen fully.
2. Apologize for inconvenience.
3. Confirm facts.
4. Check POS and item.
5. Offer approved solution or escalate.
6. Record complaint.
7. Follow up.

Control points:
- Never argue.
- Record all complaints.
- Escalate serious cases.

## SOP 14 - Hygiene and Safety

Purpose: Keep staff and branch safe.

Steps:
1. Wear PPE.
2. Check hazards.
3. Keep chemicals labeled.
4. Clean spills.
5. Keep exits clear.
6. Report incidents.

Control points:
- No chemical mixing.
- Machines operated by trained staff.
- Fire exits clear.

## SOP 15 - Emergency Response

Purpose: Protect people first.

Steps:
1. Stay calm.
2. Alert nearby staff.
3. Stop work if safe.
4. Follow emergency procedure.
5. Call supervisor/emergency number.
6. Evacuate if required.
7. Report after safe.

Control points:
- People before garments.
- No electrical contact with wet hands.
- Incident report required.
