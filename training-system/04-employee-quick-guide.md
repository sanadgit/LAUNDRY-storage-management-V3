# In & Out Laundry - Employee Quick Guide

## Daily Work Rules

- Wear clean uniform and name badge.
- Arrive on time and ready for duty.
- Follow supervisor instructions.
- Keep customer items off the floor.
- Scan items at every required stage.
- Ask when unsure.
- Report damage, missing items, safety risks, and customer complaints immediately.
- Keep phone use for approved breaks only.

## Fast SOP Reference

| Area | Key Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Reception | Count, inspect, record, confirm |
| Barcode | No item moves without tag and scan |
| Sorting | Sort by color, fabric, service, temperature |
| Washing | Correct load, cycle, detergent, temperature |
| Drying | Heat by fabric, clean lint filter |
| Pressing | Correct heat, no stains before pressing |
| Packaging | Count and verify before sealing |
| Storage | Scan exact rack or shelf location |
| Delivery | Scan before loading and verify at handover |
| Complaint | Listen, stay calm, call supervisor |
| Safety | Wear PPE and report hazards |

## Daily Workflow Summary

1. Receive task from supervisor.
2. Check tools and area.
3. Follow SOP step by step.
4. Scan item/order when required.
5. Check quality before handover.
6. Report issues early.
7. Clean area before shift end.
8. Complete handover.

## Worker Quality Questions

Before moving any item, ask:
- Is this the correct customer order?
- Is the barcode readable?
- Is the service type correct?
- Is the item clean, dry, and safe for next stage?
- Is there any damage, stain, or missing piece?

## Simple Customer Words

Use these phrases:
- "Good morning. How may I help you?"
- "Let me check and count the items with you."
- "I will record this instruction in the system."
- "Please allow me to confirm with my supervisor."
- "I am sorry for the inconvenience. We will check this for you."

Avoid these phrases:
- "I don't know."
- "Not my problem."
- "Maybe."
- "This is your fault."
- "Come later" without explanation.

## Safety Quick Rules

- Wear gloves for dirty laundry and chemicals.
- Do not mix chemicals.
- Keep wet floors marked.
- Clean lint filters.
- Do not operate machines without training.
- Never block fire exits.
- People are more important than garments in emergencies.
