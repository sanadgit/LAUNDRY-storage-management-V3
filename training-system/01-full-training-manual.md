# In & Out Laundry - Full Training Manual

Premium Laundry Operations Training System for UAE Branches

## How to Use This Manual

Use this manual for new employee onboarding, refresher training, supervisor coaching, and branch audits. Each module is written in simple operational English so staff from different nationalities can learn the same standards.

Recommended training method:
- Explain the task in the training room.
- Show photos, tools, and sample garments.
- Demonstrate the work on the production floor.
- Let the worker practice under supervision.
- Check the worker using the module checklist.
- Record the quiz result and supervisor comments.

## Brand Service Standard

In & Out Laundry must feel clean, careful, fast, and trustworthy. Every branch must protect the customer item, protect the company reputation, and keep order tracking accurate from reception to delivery.

Core service promise:
- Correct item.
- Correct service.
- Correct customer.
- Correct price.
- Correct delivery time.
- Clean and professional presentation.

## Module 2 - Customer Reception Process

### Training Objectives
- Greet customers professionally.
- Receive garments correctly.
- Record special instructions and urgent orders.

### Importance of the Task
Reception is the first quality control point. Mistakes here can cause wrong pricing, customer complaints, lost items, or late delivery.

### Required Tools & Equipment
- POS system.
- Order receipt printer.
- Garment inspection table.
- Stain tags or note labels.
- Price list.
- Barcode printer/scanner.

### Step-by-Step Workflow
1. Greet the customer with eye contact and polite words.
2. Ask for customer name and mobile number.
3. Count and inspect every item in front of the customer.
4. Identify service type: wash, press, dry clean, blanket, carpet, alteration if applicable.
5. Check stains, damage, buttons, pockets, and special instructions.
6. Enter order in POS and confirm price and delivery time.
7. Print receipt and attach order tracking.
8. Thank the customer and explain collection or delivery method.

### Standard Operating Procedures
- Never accept closed bags without opening and counting.
- Always record visible damage before processing.
- Urgent orders must be marked in POS and physically separated.
- Special instructions must be written clearly, not only spoken.

### Safety Instructions
- Wear gloves when handling dirty or wet items.
- Do not put hands inside pockets without visual check.
- Report sharp items or unsafe contents.

### Quality Standards
- Correct item count.
- Correct customer details.
- Clear service instructions.
- Accurate promised delivery time.

### Common Mistakes
- Missing stains during reception.
- Forgetting urgent order tag.
- Entering wrong customer mobile number.
- Accepting mixed services without separation.

### Troubleshooting Guide
| Issue | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Customer says item count is wrong | Recount immediately with customer present |
| POS price mismatch | Check price list and ask supervisor before payment |
| Customer requests impossible time | Explain realistic timing and offer best available slot |

### Real-World Examples
- A kandura with ink stain must be noted before washing.
- A wedding dress must be escalated for supervisor approval before acceptance.

### Do & Don't
| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Count items aloud | Guess item quantity |
| Note defects before service | Promise stain removal without checking |
| Confirm delivery date | Say "maybe" without POS update |

### Staff Productivity Tips
- Keep price categories near the counter.
- Use standard phrases for greetings and explanations.
- Prepare urgent tags before busy hours.

### Supervisor Monitoring Checklist
- Reception staff counts items correctly.
- Damage notes are visible in POS.
- Urgent orders are clearly marked.
- Receipts match physical order.

### Daily Checklist
- POS login active.
- Printer paper available.
- Barcode labels ready.
- Price list updated.
- Reception area clean.

### Quiz / Worker Questions

- Customer detail accuracy.

## Module 3 - Tagging & Barcode Workflow

### Training Objectives
- Apply barcode and QR tags correctly.
- Scan orders at each process stage.
- Prevent lost or mixed items.

### Importance of the Task
Barcode control is the main protection against missing items, wrong delivery, and customer disputes.

### Required Tools & Equipment
- Barcode printer.
- Barcode scanner.
- QR label rolls.
- Heat-safe tags if used.
- POS/order tracking system.
- Tagging gun or safe pins where approved.

### Step-by-Step Workflow
1. Match physical items with the POS order.
2. Print labels for each item or bundle according to branch policy.
3. Attach tag in approved location.
4. Scan item to confirm active status.
5. Move order to sorting area.
6. Scan at washing, drying, pressing, packaging, storage, and delivery points.

### Standard Operating Procedures
- No item moves without tag or barcode.
- Tags must not damage fabric.
- QR codes must be readable and not covered.
- If a tag falls, stop processing and escalate.

### Safety Instructions
- Keep tagging pins away from hands and customer fabric.
- Do not attach metal pins to items going into machines if not approved.
- Keep scanner cords and charging stations tidy.

### Quality Standards
- 100 percent barcode readability.
- Correct tag placement.
- Every status update scanned on time.
- No duplicate or manual-only order movement.

### Common Mistakes
- Tagging the wrong garment.
- Placing barcode on wet or curved surface.
- Forgetting to scan after packaging.
- Reusing old labels.

### Troubleshooting Guide
| Issue | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Barcode not scanning | Clean scanner, reprint label, update POS note |
| Label damaged during wash | Match order using backup tag and supervisor verification |
| Duplicate tag found | Stop order and audit all items before processing |

### Real-World Examples
- A blanket receives one large-item barcode and storage location scan.
- A customer order with 10 shirts must show 10 linked line items or an approved bundle process.

### Do & Don't
| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Scan before moving zones | Carry unscanned items |
| Reprint unreadable labels | Write random numbers by hand |
| Place tags on approved areas | Attach tags to delicate visible areas |

### Staff Productivity Tips
- Scan batches in the same physical order every time.
- Keep spare label rolls near the tagging station.
- Separate problem items immediately.

### Supervisor Monitoring Checklist
- Random barcode scan audit passed.
- Untagged item area is empty.
- Tag placement follows standards.
- Exception log is updated.

### Daily Checklist
- Scanner charged.
- Printer tested.
- Label roll available.
- POS sync working.
- Exception tray empty.

### Quiz / Worker Questions

### KPI Metrics
- Scan compliance rate.
- Lost item count.

### Visual Slide Suggestions

## Module 4 - Clothes Sorting Operations

### Training Objectives
- Sort garments by color, fabric, service, temperature, and customer category.
- Identify stains and delicate items before washing.

### Importance of the Task
Correct sorting protects fabric, prevents color transfer, improves wash quality, and reduces rewash.

### Required Tools & Equipment
- Sorting bins.
- Color-coded baskets.
- Stain identification chart.
- Gloves.
- Delicate garment bags.
- Service labels.

### Step-by-Step Workflow
1. Scan order into sorting area.
2. Separate by service type.
3. Sort by color: white, light, dark, bright, risk color.
4. Sort by fabric: cotton, wool, silk, synthetic, delicate, heavy.
5. Check stains and mark pre-treatment.
6. Separate urgent and VIP orders.
7. Send sorted batches to correct washing or pressing station.

### Standard Operating Procedures
- Whites and darks must never be washed together.
- Delicate and high-value items require supervisor check.
- Stained items must be treated before wash.
- Customer order integrity must be maintained with tracking.

### Safety Instructions
- Use gloves for dirty items.
- Do not lift overloaded baskets.
- Keep sorting floor clear and dry.

### Quality Standards
- No color bleeding.
- No fabric damage from wrong cycle.
- Correct service routing.
- Stains identified before washing.

### Common Mistakes
- Mixing white shirts with colored items.
- Sending dry-clean-only garments to wash.
- Missing oil or ink stains.
- Overloading sorting baskets.

### Troubleshooting Guide
| Issue | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Unsure fabric type | Pause and ask supervisor |
| Strong stain found | Mark stain, photo if required, send to pre-treatment |
| Mixed customer items | Scan and separate before next stage |

### Real-World Examples
- A red dress must be tested and separated from white garments.
- Delicate abaya embroidery must go into a protective bag or specialist process.

### Do & Don't

| Sort before washing | Rush items directly to machine |

### Staff Productivity Tips

### Supervisor Monitoring Checklist

## Module 5 - Washing Procedures

### Training Objectives
- Prepare machines safely.
- Select detergent, chemicals, temperature, and wash cycle correctly.
- Protect fabric quality.

### Importance of the Task
Washing is the main cleaning stage. Wrong chemicals, heat, or loading can damage customer items and increase costs.

### Required Tools & Equipment
- Washing machines.
- Approved detergents and chemicals.
- Measuring cups or dosing system.
- PPE gloves and eye protection.
- Machine load chart.
- Fabric care guide.

### Step-by-Step Workflow
1. Check machine cleanliness and readiness.
2. Confirm sorted batch and scan status.
3. Check fabric care and soil level.
4. Load machine within weight limit.
5. Select correct program and temperature.
6. Add approved detergent or confirm auto-dosing.
7. Start cycle and monitor first minutes.
8. Remove items quickly after cycle ends.
9. Send to drying or next stage with scan update.

### Standard Operating Procedures
- Never overload machine.
- Use only approved chemicals.
- Follow temperature rules by fabric.
- Record machine issues immediately.

### Safety Instructions
- Wear PPE when handling chemicals.
- Never mix chemicals unless approved.
- Do not open machines during unsafe cycle.
- Keep wet floor signs available.

### Quality Standards
- Clean smell without strong chemical odor.
- No detergent residue.
- No fabric shrinkage or damage.
- Correct cycle completed and scanned.

### Common Mistakes
- Too much detergent.
- Wrong temperature for delicate fabric.
- Overloading machine.
- Leaving wet clothes inside too long.

### Troubleshooting Guide
| Issue | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Clothes still dirty | Check load size, detergent dose, cycle, and stain pre-treatment |
| Machine alarm | Stop safely and report machine code |
| Strong smell | Run approved rinse process and check dosing |

### Real-World Examples
- Cotton towels can use stronger cycles than delicate blouses.
- Wool items require low temperature and special handling.

### Do & Don't
| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Follow machine load chart | Fill machine by force |
| Use approved dosing | Pour chemicals by guess |
| Check care labels | Treat all fabrics the same |

### Staff Productivity Tips
- Group similar loads to reduce machine changes.
- Prepare next load while current load runs.
- Report small machine issues before they become breakdowns.

### Supervisor Monitoring Checklist
- Correct program selected.
- Load weight controlled.
- Chemical storage safe.
- Machine logs updated.

### Daily Checklist
- Machines checked.
- Chemicals labeled.
- PPE available.
- Load chart visible.
- Floor dry and safe.

### Quiz / Worker Questions

### KPI Metrics

## Module 7 - Drying Procedures

### Training Objectives
- Select dryer settings correctly.
- Prevent shrinkage, overheating, and fabric damage.
- Confirm dryness quality.

### Importance of the Task
Drying affects fabric size, feel, smell, and finishing speed. Wrong heat can damage garments permanently.

### Required Tools & Equipment
- Dryers.
- Dryer load chart.
- Lint filter brush.
- Dryness check table.
- Heat setting guide.

### Step-by-Step Workflow
1. Scan batch into drying stage.
2. Check fabric type and wash status.
3. Clean lint filter.
4. Load dryer within limit.
5. Select heat and time.
6. Check during cycle for delicate or heavy items.
7. Remove items when dry.
8. Separate items for ironing, folding, or packaging.

### Standard Operating Procedures
- Clean lint filters before each shift and as needed.
- Use low heat for delicate or shrink-risk items.
- Never dry items with chemical smell without supervisor check.
- Do not mix heavy wet items with light garments.

### Safety Instructions
- Lint build-up is a fire risk.
- Do not touch hot metal areas.
- Keep dryer area clear.
- Report burning smell immediately.

### Quality Standards
- Items fully dry but not overheated.
- No shrinkage.
- No burnt smell.
- Items moved quickly to prevent wrinkles.

### Common Mistakes
- High heat for all items.
- Ignoring lint filters.
- Overdrying.
- Leaving dry garments in dryer.

### Troubleshooting Guide
| Issue | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Items still wet | Separate heavy items and run additional controlled cycle |
| Dryer overheating | Stop use and report to supervisor |
| Strong wrinkles | Move quickly to pressing and adjust drying time |

### Real-World Examples
- Cotton towels can take higher heat than synthetic uniforms.
- Delicate abayas may need air drying or low heat approval.

### Do & Don't

### Supervisor Monitoring Checklist
- Emergency contacts posted.
- Fire exits clear.
- Staff drill completed.
- Incident report filed.

### Daily Checklist
- Exits clear.
- Fire equipment visible.
- First aid kit accessible.
- Spill kit ready.
- Emergency numbers posted.

### Quiz / Worker Questions
- What is the first priority in an emergency?
- What should you do during a power outage?
- Who should report a machine malfunction?

### KPI Metrics
- Drill completion.
- Emergency response time.
- Near miss reporting.
- Safety inspection score.

### Visual Slide Suggestions
- Emergency flowchart.
- Branch evacuation map.
- Fire and chemical accident response cards.

### Suggested Icons & Images
- Icons: siren, fire extinguisher, first aid, water drop, power.
- Images: evacuation route, emergency contact board, spill kit.

### Short Summary
Emergencies require calm action, clear reporting, and people-first decisions.

## Manual Closing Standard

Every branch should use the same training language, checklists, and KPI definitions. Supervisors should coach staff daily using real examples from the branch. Training is complete only when the employee can perform the task safely, correctly, and consistently during real work.
