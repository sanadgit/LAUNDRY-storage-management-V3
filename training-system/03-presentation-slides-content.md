# In & Out Laundry - Presentation Slides Content

## Design Direction

Use a premium UAE corporate style:
- Background: white or very light grey.
- Main color: deep navy.
- Accent colors: teal, gold, and safety red.
- Fonts: clean sans-serif.
- Layout: large photo, short bullets, simple workflow diagrams.
- Avoid crowded slides.

## Module Slide Pattern

For each module, use 3 to 5 slides:
- Slide A: What the task is and why it matters.
- Slide B: Step-by-step workflow.
- Slide C: Quality and safety standards.
- Slide D: Common mistakes and troubleshooting.
- Slide E: Supervisor checklist and KPI.

## Slide Layout Library

Use these repeatable layouts:
- Workflow slide: title, five process blocks, one quality reminder.
- Photo checklist slide: large real photo with 4 to 6 callouts.
- Do and don't slide: two clean columns with icons.
- Supervisor audit slide: checklist table plus KPI box.
- Dashboard slide: top KPI cards, trend chart, open action list.

## Speaker Notes Standard

Each slide should have short trainer notes:
- Explain the point in simple words.
- Give one real branch example.
- Ask one worker question.
- Demonstrate the task if possible.

## Sample Slide Copy

### Slide: Barcode Rule

Title: No Scan, No Movement

Bullets:
- Every customer item must have an identity.
- Scan before moving to the next stage.
- If the label is missing, stop and call the supervisor.
- Do not guess customer or order number.

Visual:
- Large barcode image with process arrows.

Trainer question:
- What should you do if a barcode does not scan?

### Slide: Complaint Handling

Title: Stay Calm and Solve

Bullets:
- Listen first.
- Apologize for the inconvenience.
- Check facts in POS.
- Offer approved solution.
- Record and follow up.

Visual:
- Five-step conversation flow.
